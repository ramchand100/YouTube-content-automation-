## Blocking items before `status: production-ready`

Per `.claude/rules/source-audits.md`, every claim must be Confirmed or Removed — no
"Needs verification" or "Conflicting" claim may remain. The script's own `[VERIFY]`
tags already flag most of the "Needs verification" items above for pre-recording
checks, which is consistent with this audit. Two items are new and more serious
than a routine VERIFY, because they are stated as settled figures without a tag:

1. **C002 (Rs 19 billion SSGC dues)** — currently written as a plain `[SOURCE]` claim
   with no hedge, but the figure conflicts with Dawn's own direct headline reporting
   (~Rs35bn) from the same week. This should be re-tagged `[VERIFY]` or revised now,
   not left for the pre-recording pass — it is a specific number a viewer could
   fact-check in ten seconds.
2. **C021 (>Rs 20 billion annual interest)** — already has a `[VERIFY]` tag, which is
   correct, but the only source located in this audit is Rs17bn, meaningfully lower.
   Recommend the pre-recording verification pass start from the ProPakistani Rs17bn
   figure rather than searching blind for a source above Rs20bn.

All other "Needs verification" items are already correctly flagged `[VERIFY]` in the
script and can proceed through the normal pre-recording verification pass described
in the script's front matter (`freshness_status`).

**Recommendation:** Script remains `status: draft`. It should not move to
`production-ready` until C002 and C021 are resolved (revise the figures or the
attribution) and the pre-recording `[VERIFY]` pass clears the remaining seven items.
