# Thumbnail Concepts & AI Prompts — Episode 04
## "Why Poor Families Pay for a Free Education"

Two high-CTR thumbnail concepts following the channel rules: black-based palette,
green/white text, **one** focal point, **2-4 words** that add a new idea rather than
repeat the title, and readability at small feed size. Dignity first.

The title already carries "free" and "pay", so the thumbnail should show the
*mechanism*, the choice between a locked free school and a paid one, not repeat those
words.

**Palette:** background `#0A0A0A` · text white `#F5F5F5` · accent green `#1EB53A`
(sparingly) · alert red `#FB5A5A` (the failure/gap). **Font:** bold condensed sans
(Coolvetica). **Aspect:** 1280x720. Test at 320px wide.

---

## CONCEPT A — "The Locked Gate" (walking past free)

**Big idea:** the whole paradox in one frame. A padlocked, empty government school
gate in the foreground; a parent and child walking past it toward a small hand-painted
private school sign. The viewer instantly asks: why walk past the free one?

**Composition:**
- Foreground right: a rusted, chained government school gate, clearly shut. A faded
  official "Govt School" board.
- Mid-left: a parent holding a child's hand, walking away from it, toward a small
  private-school signboard.
- Dark, simple sky. Heavy black space top-left for text.
- Optional: a small red "FREE" tag on the locked gate, to sharpen the irony.

**Text (on-thumbnail):** `WALKING PAST FREE` — white, with `FREE` in red, top-left,
two lines. Blunt and curious.

**Why it works:** it shows the exact anomaly the video explains, a shut free school and
a family choosing to pay, without repeating the title's words. One clear story, reads
at small size.

**Midjourney prompt:**
```
Cinematic photograph of a rusted padlocked government school gate with a chain across
it in the foreground on a quiet Pakistani street at morning, a faded official school
board, and in the mid-ground a parent holding a young child's hand walking away toward
a small hand-painted private school sign, muted documentary tone, soft morning light,
photorealistic, high detail, dark negative space in the upper left for text
--ar 16:9 --style raw --v 6.1
```
Negative / avoid: `--no text, words, identifiable faces, logos, watermark, clutter`

## CONCEPT B — "Two Classrooms" (the choice, side by side)

**Big idea:** the comparison that drives the whole video, in one split frame. On one
side, an empty, broken government classroom (no teacher, broken fan). On the other, a
cramped but full private classroom with a teacher present. It shows what the fee buys:
presence.

**Composition:**
- Vertical split down the middle. Left: a bare, empty government classroom, a broken
  fan, dust. Right: a small crowded private classroom, kids at desks, a teacher writing.
- Keep both slightly dark and real, not staged-perfect.
- A thin vertical divider line, faint red on the empty side, faint green on the full side.

**Text (on-thumbnail):** `SAME PRICE?` is wrong here, use `EMPTY vs FULL` — white, with
`vs` small, centered on the divider, one line. Or simply `WHERE'S THE TEACHER?` lower-left.

**Why it works:** the side-by-side instantly frames the trade-off (free-but-empty vs
paid-but-working) and poses the question the title answers. Strong contrast, one idea,
scannable small.

**Midjourney prompt:**
```
Split-screen documentary photograph, left half an empty neglected government classroom
in Pakistan with a broken ceiling fan, cracked walls and no teacher, right half a small
crowded low-cost private classroom with children at wooden desks and a teacher writing
on a board, both dimly lit and realistic, strong tonal contrast between the two halves,
photorealistic, high detail, space for a short caption --ar 16:9 --style raw --v 6.1
```
Negative / avoid: `--no added text, words, identifiable faces, brand logos, watermark`

## A/B test plan
- Ship **Concept A** (the locked gate) as primary: it carries the curiosity gap in a
  single glance and travels well in the browse feed. Hold **Concept B** as the swap if
  48-hour CTR underperforms baseline.
- Keep the title constant so the test isolates the thumbnail.
- Maintain the channel signature: one accent word plus one strong hero image.

## Editor / dignity notes
- Generate at 1280x720 or larger; export final under 2 MB.
- Set on-thumbnail text in the channel font (Coolvetica), not an AI render.
- No identifiable real children's faces. Use wide framing, backs of heads, and staged
  recreations so the thumbnail is honest without exploiting anyone.
- Reserve green for the hopeful in-video beats; these thumbnails lean white/red.
