# Thumbnail Concepts & AI Prompts — Episode 01
## "The $500 Billion Trap: Why Pakistan's Wealth Is Locked in Real Estate"

Two high-CTR thumbnail concepts. Both follow the channel rules: black-based
palette, green/white text, **one** focal point, **2-4 words** of text that add a
new idea rather than repeat the title, and readability at small feed size.

The title carries the words "$500 Billion" and "Real Estate", so neither thumbnail
should repeat those. The thumbnail's job is to open a curiosity gap the title does
not close.

**Palette:** background `#0A0A0A` · text white `#F5F5F5` · accent green `#1EB53A`
· alert red `#FB5A5A` (sparingly). **Font:** bold condensed sans (Coolvetica).
**Aspect:** 1280x720 (16:9). Test every version as a 320px-wide thumbnail before finalizing.

---

## CONCEPT A — "The Empty City" (curiosity + unease)

**Big idea:** perfect, expensive, and completely empty. The visual says "something
is wrong here" without explaining what. The viewer clicks to find out why all this
wealth built nothing.

**Composition:**
- Focal point: a wide, slightly elevated view of an immaculate but deserted housing
  society at golden-hour, roads and streetlights present, houses absent. Hero sits
  right-of-center.
- Left third kept darker and simpler for text. Heavy black gradient up from the
  bottom for legibility.
- One human element for scale and story: a single tiny silhouetted figure standing
  alone on an empty road, dwarfed by the emptiness.

**Text (on-thumbnail):** `NO ONE LIVES HERE` — white, with `NO ONE` in green.
Upper-left, three short stacked lines. That is the entire copy.

**Optional graphic accent:** a small red upward price-arrow tag reading `SOLD 7X`
pinned near an empty plot. Adds the "traded but empty" tension in one glance.

**Why it works:** the contradiction (beautiful, developed, sold repeatedly, yet
empty and lifeless) is the curiosity gap. It never says the word "trap" or a
number, so it complements the title instead of echoing it.

**Midjourney prompt:**
```
Cinematic wide aerial photograph of a pristine but completely empty modern housing
society in Pakistan at golden hour, immaculate paved roads, streetlights on,
roundabouts and boundary walls, almost no houses built, one lone tiny human
silhouette standing on an empty road for scale, moody desaturated teal-and-amber
grade, deep shadows, dramatic low sun, high detail, photorealistic, strong negative
space on the left for text, dark cinematic vignette --ar 16:9 --style raw --v 6.1
```
Negative / avoid: `--no text, words, logos, crowds, cars, greenery overload`

## CONCEPT B — "Paper Over Concrete" (the file reveal)

**Big idea:** make the abstract "file" physical. A single ordinary paper document
is the hero, dwarfing a real building behind it, priced like gold. It visualizes
the core anomaly of the episode: paper worth more than what it is supposed to become.

**Composition:**
- Focal point: a plain manila property "file"/document held up in the foreground,
  crisp and oversized, occupying the right-center. On it, a faint stamped plot
  outline and a green rising price line.
- Background: a real, out-of-focus concrete apartment block or plot boundary,
  darkened and blurred so the paper clearly wins the frame.
- A subtle green upward graph line runs from the building into the paper, implying
  value flowing into the document, not the structure.

**Text (on-thumbnail):** `JUST PAPER?` — white with `PAPER` in green, lower-left,
two lines. The question mark is the hook.

**Optional graphic accent:** a small red `+OWN` premium tag clipped to the file
corner (the real market term, defined in the script), teasing an insider detail.

**Why it works:** it turns the invisible mechanic (a file, a paper promise) into a
single striking object and poses a question the title does not answer. High
contrast, one clear hero, tiny word count, and it reads at thumbnail size.

**Midjourney prompt:**
```
Dramatic product-style photograph of a single plain manila property document held
upright in the foreground, oversized and sharp, faint stamped land-plot outline and
a glowing green rising price line printed on it, blurred darkened concrete apartment
building behind it out of focus, near-black background, strong single spotlight on
the paper, high contrast, photorealistic, cinematic, generous dark negative space on
the lower left for text --ar 16:9 --style raw --v 6.1
```
Negative / avoid: `--no text, words, watermark, logos, cluttered background`

## A/B test plan
- Ship **Concept A** as the primary (emotion + curiosity travels best in the browse
  feed); hold **Concept B** as the swap if 48-hour CTR underperforms the channel baseline.
- Keep the same title for both so the test isolates the thumbnail.
- Whichever wins, keep a recurring signature across future thumbnails (green accent
  word + one bold hero object) so the channel stays recognizable in a feed.

## Editor handoff notes
- Generate at 1280x720 or larger; export final under 2 MB (YouTube limit).
- Set on-thumbnail text in the actual channel font (Coolvetica), not an AI render,
  so type stays crisp and consistent. Midjourney supplies the image only.
- Confirm faces/props do not resemble any real, identifiable person or company.
