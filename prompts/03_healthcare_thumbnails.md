# Thumbnail Concepts & AI Prompts — Episode 03
## "Getting Sick Can Make You Poor: Inside Pakistan's Cash Healthcare Trap"

Two high-CTR thumbnail concepts following the channel rules: black-based palette,
green/white text, **one** focal point, **2-4 words** that add a new idea rather than
repeat the title, and readability at small feed size.

This topic is human and heavy, so the thumbnails must create curiosity without
mocking real hardship. Dignity first. The title carries "sick" and "poor", so the
thumbnail should show the *mechanism* (cash, medicine, the trade-off), not repeat
those words.

**Palette:** background `#0A0A0A` · text white `#F5F5F5` · accent green `#1EB53A`
(reserved, used sparingly) · alert red `#FB5A5A` (cost/loss). **Font:** bold
condensed sans (Coolvetica). **Aspect:** 1280x720. Test at 320px wide.

---

## CONCEPT A — "Pills or Rupees" (the impossible trade-off)

**Big idea:** the everyday choice at the heart of the episode, made physical. One
open palm holds a few loose medicine tablets; the other holds a small fold of
banknotes. The image says "you can afford one, not both" without a word of the title.

**Composition:**
- Two open hands, palms up, entering from the bottom of the frame. Left palm: loose
  tablets. Right palm: a thin fold of rupee notes. Balanced, slightly off-center.
- Dark, simple background. Strong top-down light on the hands. Heavy black space at
  the top for text.
- Optional tiny red arrow/scale motif between the hands to imply the trade-off.

**Text (on-thumbnail):** `PILLS OR FOOD?` — white, with `OR` small in red, top-center,
one line. Short, blunt, and it reframes the choice bigger than the title does.

**Why it works:** it turns an abstract economic problem into one human decision.
Curiosity plus stakes, no melodrama, reads instantly at small size. It adds a new
idea (the trade-off) the title never states.

**Midjourney prompt:**
```
Cinematic top-down photograph of two open human hands entering from the bottom of the
frame against a near-black background, the left palm holding a few loose medicine
tablets, the right palm holding a small thin fold of Pakistani rupee banknotes, single
soft overhead light, shallow depth of field, muted respectful tone, high detail,
photorealistic, generous dark negative space at the top for text --ar 16:9 --style raw --v 6.1
```
Negative / avoid: `--no text, words, faces, logos, watermark, clutter`

---

## CONCEPT B — "The Empty Wallet at the Counter" (the moment of truth)

**Big idea:** the exact instant the money runs out. A worn wallet held open over a
pharmacy or hospital counter, clearly near-empty, a strip of medicine just out of
reach. It captures the trap in one frozen moment.

**Composition:**
- Foreground: hands holding an open, near-empty wallet over a counter. On the counter,
  a strip of tablets or a bill, slightly out of focus.
- Background: a softly blurred pharmacy/hospital counter, darkened. Keep the wallet the
  clear hero.
- A faint red glow on the bill/price; keep everything else muted.

**Text (on-thumbnail):** `WHEN IT RUNS OUT` — white with `OUT` in red, lower-left,
two lines. The unfinished feeling of the phrase is the hook.

**Why it works:** it freezes the emotional peak of the episode (the money finishing
before the treatment does) into a single, quiet, relatable image. One hero object,
tiny word count, works small.

**Midjourney prompt:**
```
Dramatic close-up photograph of worn hands holding an open, nearly empty wallet over a
pharmacy counter, a strip of medicine tablets resting on the counter slightly out of
focus, dark moody background, one soft side light, faint red tint on the medicine,
shallow depth of field, respectful documentary tone, photorealistic, dark negative
space in the lower left for text --ar 16:9 --style raw --v 6.1
```
Negative / avoid: `--no text, words, identifiable faces, brand logos, watermark`

## A/B test plan
- Ship **Concept A** as primary (the trade-off framing travels furthest and is the
  clearest curiosity gap); hold **Concept B** as the swap if 48-hour CTR underperforms.
- Keep the title constant so the test isolates the thumbnail.
- Maintain the channel signature: one accent word plus one strong hero image.

## Editor / dignity notes
- Generate at 1280x720 or larger; export final under 2 MB.
- Set on-thumbnail text in the channel font (Coolvetica), not an AI render.
- No identifiable real patient faces. Use hands, objects, and staged recreations so the
  thumbnail is honest without exploiting anyone.
- Reserve green; on this episode the thumbnails lean white/red, keeping green for the
  in-video solution beats.
