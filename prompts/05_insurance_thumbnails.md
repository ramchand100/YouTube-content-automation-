# Thumbnail Concepts & AI Prompts — Episode 05
## "Why a Country of 240 Million Won't Buy Insurance"

Two high-CTR thumbnail concepts following the channel rules: black-based palette,
green/white text, **one** focal point, **2-4 words** that add a new idea rather than
repeat the title, and readability at small feed size. Handle the topic with respect;
no imagery that mocks faith.

The title already carries "insurance" and the scale, so the thumbnail should show the
*consequence* of having none, not repeat the word.

**Palette:** background `#0A0A0A` · text white `#F5F5F5` · accent green `#1EB53A`
(the shared pool / safety, used sparingly) · alert red `#FB5A5A` (loss/exposure).
**Font:** bold condensed sans (Coolvetica). **Aspect:** 1280x720. Test at 320px wide.

---

## CONCEPT A — "No Safety Net" (the fall)

**Big idea:** the whole point of insurance in one image. A single figure falling, with
an empty space where a safety net should be. It says "nothing is there to catch you"
without a word of the title.

**Composition:**
- Center-right: a small silhouetted figure mid-fall against a dark void.
- Below, a frayed, torn safety net with a gaping hole, faintly red at the edges.
- Heavy black negative space top-left for text. Keep it stark and simple.
- Optional: a faint green intact net just out of reach, to hint at what could exist.

**Text (on-thumbnail):** `NOTHING CATCHES YOU` — white, with `NOTHING` in red,
top-left, two lines. Blunt, high-stakes, curious.

**Why it works:** it turns an abstract financial idea (no insurance) into a physical,
visceral image of falling with no net. One clear metaphor, tiny word count, reads
instantly at small size. It never says "insurance," so it complements the title.

**Midjourney prompt:**
```
Cinematic conceptual photograph of a single small silhouetted human figure falling
through a dark empty void, far below a frayed torn safety net with a large hole in the
middle, faint red rim light on the torn edges, deep shadows, minimal, high contrast,
photorealistic, dramatic, generous black negative space in the upper left for text
--ar 16:9 --style raw --v 6.1
```
Negative / avoid: `--no text, words, faces, logos, watermark, clutter`

---

## CONCEPT B — "Gold Instead" (the real safety net)

**Big idea:** show what Pakistanis use instead of insurance. A pair of hands cradling a
small stack of gold bangles, held close and protective, against black. It captures the
episode's key insight: the family safety net is gold and each other, not a policy.

**Composition:**
- Center: worn hands cupping a few gold bangles, held protectively, warm light on the
  gold against a near-black background.
- Keep it intimate and dignified, not flashy. More than half the frame dark.
- Optional: a faint red downward arrow or crack behind the hands, hinting the net is
  thin.

**Text (on-thumbnail):** `THIS IS THE PLAN` — white, with `PLAN` in green, lower-left,
two lines. The irony (gold as the entire financial plan) is the hook.

**Why it works:** it reframes the story around what people actually do, which is more
surprising and human than a chart. One hero object, strong contrast, small word count,
and it teases the real-safety-net idea the title never states.

**Midjourney prompt:**
```
Intimate close-up photograph of a pair of worn hands cupping a small stack of gold
bangles held protectively against a near-black background, warm directional light on
the gold, shallow depth of field, respectful dignified tone, photorealistic, high
detail, dark negative space in the lower left for text --ar 16:9 --style raw --v 6.1
```
Negative / avoid: `--no text, words, faces, brand logos, watermark, busy background`

## A/B test plan
- Ship **Concept A** (no safety net) as primary: the falling-figure metaphor is the
  clearest, highest-stakes curiosity gap and travels well in the browse feed. Hold
  **Concept B** as the swap if 48-hour CTR underperforms baseline.
- Keep the title constant so the test isolates the thumbnail.
- Maintain the channel signature: one accent word plus one strong hero image.

## Editor / dignity notes
- Generate at 1280x720 or larger; export final under 2 MB.
- Set on-thumbnail text in the channel font (Coolvetica), not an AI render.
- No identifiable real faces. Keep the faith angle out of the thumbnail entirely; it is
  handled respectfully inside the video, not as a hook.
- Reserve green for the "safety/what-could-be" cue; these thumbnails lean white/red.
