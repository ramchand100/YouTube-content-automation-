# Thumbnail Concepts & AI Prompts — Episode 02
## "Why Pakistan Still Pays to Move Money When Raast Is Free"

Two high-CTR thumbnail concepts following the channel rules: black-based palette,
green/white text, **one** focal point, **2-4 words** that add a new idea rather than
repeat the title, and readability at small feed size.

The title already carries "Raast" and "Free", so the thumbnail should not repeat
either. Its job is to make the paradox visual: a free rail sitting unused while
money still leaks to fees.

**Palette:** background `#0A0A0A` · text white `#F5F5F5` · accent green `#1EB53A`
(Raast/free) · alert red `#FB5A5A` (fees/leak). **Font:** bold condensed sans
(Coolvetica). **Aspect:** 1280x720. Test every version at 320px wide before finalizing.

---

## CONCEPT A — "The Untouched Sticker" (curiosity + specificity)

**Big idea:** the free option is right there, physically ignored. A close, real
Raast-style QR sticker on a grubby shop counter, gathering dust, while a hand reaches
past it with cash. The image says "free money, refused" without a word of the title.

**Composition:**
- Focal point: a QR sticker on a counter, sharp and well-lit, right-of-center.
- A hand enters from the left holding banknotes, deliberately bypassing the sticker.
- Left third darkened for text. Heavy black gradient from the bottom.
- Optional: a small green `FREE` tag on the sticker vs a red price-tag `2%` floating
  over the cash, so the contradiction reads instantly.

**Text (on-thumbnail):** `NOBODY SCANS IT` — white, with `NOBODY` in green,
upper-left, two lines. That is the whole copy.

**Why it works:** it poses a question the title answers. A free, official payment
tool sitting unused is a genuine curiosity gap, and the cash-past-the-QR gesture
tells the whole story in one glance. Reads clean at thumbnail size.

**Midjourney prompt:**
```
Cinematic close-up photograph of a QR-code payment sticker taped to a worn Pakistani
shop counter, slightly faded and curling at one corner, sharp focus, warm practical
lighting, a hand entering frame from the left holding rupee banknotes and reaching
past the sticker, shallow depth of field, moody dark background with strong negative
space on the upper left for text, photorealistic, high detail --ar 16:9 --style raw --v 6.1
```
Negative / avoid: `--no text, words, brand logos, watermark, clutter`

---

## CONCEPT B — "The Leak" (money-flow metaphor)

**Big idea:** visualize the fee leak. A single rupee coin or note with a thin stream
of small coins draining out of it, flowing off-frame, tinted red. It makes the
invisible 2% into something you can see bleeding away.

**Composition:**
- Focal point: one oversized rupee note or coin, centered slightly right, lit hard
  against near-black.
- A fine stream of small coins leaks from its edge and trails off toward the frame
  edge, tinted red (the fee leaving). A faint green "0%" glow sits on the opposite
  side, unused.
- Keep more than half the frame dark and simple.

**Text (on-thumbnail):** `WHERE IT LEAKS` — white with `LEAKS` in red, lower-left,
two lines. The word "leaks" carries the tension.

**Why it works:** it turns an abstract fee into a physical loss, which is more
clickable than any chart. High contrast, one hero object, tiny word count. The red
leak plus the ignored green "0%" previews the whole thesis of the episode.

**Midjourney prompt:**
```
Dramatic macro photograph of a single large Pakistani rupee coin standing upright on
a reflective near-black surface, a fine stream of tiny coins leaking from its edge and
trailing off toward the frame edge, subtle red rim-light on the leaking stream, one
hard spotlight, deep shadows, high contrast, photorealistic, cinematic, generous dark
negative space in the lower left for text --ar 16:9 --style raw --v 6.1
```
Negative / avoid: `--no text, words, watermark, logos, busy background`

## A/B test plan
- Ship **Concept A** as primary (the human gesture and specificity travel best in the
  browse feed); hold **Concept B** as the swap if 48-hour CTR underperforms baseline.
- Keep the title constant across both so the test isolates the thumbnail.
- Maintain the channel signature: one green accent word plus one bold hero object.

## Editor handoff notes
- Generate at 1280x720 or larger; export final under 2 MB.
