# Research Brief — The LNG Trap: How Pakistan Got Hooked on Imported Gas

- **Episode:** 06
- **Pillar:** Macro
- **Drafted:** 2026-08-19

---

## The Central Question

Pakistan discovered gas at Sui, Balochistan, in 1952 and built its industrial economy on cheap domestic gas. Fifty years of suppressed prices killed exploration investment, domestic production peaked around 2012 and has been declining since, and Pakistan is now structurally dependent on Liquefied Natural Gas imported through two Karachi terminals and priced in US dollars. In January 2022, Pakistan issued a spot LNG tender and received zero responses. How did a gas-rich country end up unable to buy gas on the global market, and why does the bill keep growing regardless of which government is in office?

## Key Actors

- **OGRA** — Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority; sets consumer tariffs
- **PLL** — Pakistan LNG Limited; state procurement entity
- **SNGPL** — Sui Northern Gas Pipelines; distribution for Punjab and KPK
- **SSGCL** — Sui Southern Gas Company; distribution for Sindh and Balochistan
- **EETPL** — Engro Elengy Terminal; one of two Port Qasim re-gas terminals
- **PGPCL / PGPC** — Pakistan GasPort; second Port Qasim terminal
- **Qatar Energy** — Pakistan's largest long-term LNG supplier
- **Ministry of Energy (Petroleum Division)** — policy authority
