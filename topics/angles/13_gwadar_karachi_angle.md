## Broad subject

Karachi Port and Port Qasim, two seaports sharing roughly 40 kilometres of Karachi coastline, together handle the large majority of Pakistan's seaborne trade — making them the physical gateway most of the country's imports and exports pass through, and making Gwadar's continued low share of that trade a specific, testable question rather than a vague impression.
