# Storyboard & Motion-Graphic Blueprint — Episode 06
## "The LNG Trap: How Pakistan Got Hooked on Imported Gas"

Timestamp-linked to `scripts/06_the_lng_trap.md`. Faceless format: voiceover +
motion graphics + B-roll only. Built for a solo editor in **After Effects**
(motion graphics) and **Premiere Pro** (assembly).

Every cue is labelled GRAPHIC or FOOTAGE and specifies:
- What appears and where on the canvas
- Step-by-step build sequence with sub-timecodes
- How long each element holds after building
- Exact end point and transition type

**Canvas:** 1920×1080, near-black `#0A0A0A` background.
**Palette:** white `#F5F5F5` · green `#1EB53A` · red `#FB5A5A` · dim grey `#666666`.
**Type:** Coolvetica / Archivo Black for numbers and titles (bold, condensed) ·
Inter for captions, definition cards, and source citations.
**Source caption style:** Inter, 11px, white at 60% opacity, bottom-right corner,
present for as long as any sourced number is visible on screen.
**Music:** ambient, low. Drops to silence under key analytical conclusions.
Resumes quietly after the pause.

## Global production notes
- Every on-screen number has a source caption present for the full duration
  the number is visible. Remove only when the number is no longer on screen.
- All [VERIFY] figures must be confirmed from OGRA, SBP, PBS, or PLL
  primary sources before any graphic is rendered. Do not render stale numbers.
- Colour language: green = domestic production, long-term stability, forward
  paths. Red = cost gaps, circular debt, spot price risk. Keep this consistent
  so the viewer reads colour without thinking after two episodes.
- Music drops to silence at 11:50 (trap summary) and 12:30 (verdict). These
  are the two most important lines in the episode. Let them land clean.
- The domino chain animation (macroRipple) should feel mechanical, not dramatic.
  Simple blocks, clean falls, no camera shake or sound effects. Let the
  chain logic do the work.
- Export: 1080p minimum, 4K preferred. Audio: -14 LUFS integrated, -1 dBTP.
