# Storyboard & Motion-Graphic Blueprint — Episode 01
## "The $500 Billion Trap: Why Pakistan's Wealth Is Locked in Real Estate"

Timestamp-linked to `scripts/01_pakistan_real_estate_trap.md`. Built for a solo
editor in **After Effects** (motion graphics) and **Premiere Pro** (assembly).
Nothing here needs 3D or a team. Reusable elements are defined once at the bottom.

**Palette:** background near-black `#0A0A0A`; primary text white `#F5F5F5`; accent
green `#1EB53A` for key words and positive/rising values; alert red `#FB5A5A` for
loss, cash-leak, and "dead" states. **Type:** bold condensed sans (Coolvetica or
Archivo Black) for numbers and titles; clean sans (Inter) for captions.

---

## PART 1 — The Hook (0:00 - 2:00)

**[00:00] Empty society aerial.**
- Footage: licensed drone shot of a fully-developed-but-empty housing society at
  dusk (roads, streetlights, roundabouts, near-zero houses). If no stock, build a
  slow 2.5D parallax push over a high-res satellite still (AE camera + displacement).
- Grade: cool, slightly desaturated. Vignette in. Let it breathe, no text 0:00-0:20.

**[00:35] "FILE" reveal.**
- Freeze-frame the aerial (frame hold), push slightly out of focus (Camera Lens Blur).
- Slide in a single document graphic center-frame, label `FILE` in green.
- To its right, a price ticker counts up: `50,00,000 → 55,00,000 → 62,00,000` (use a
  Slider-driven number, monospaced). Small upward "OWN +" flashes on each tick.

**[01:20] Title card.**
- Cut to pure black. Type-on: **THE $500 BILLION TRAP**, condensed, white with
  green on "TRAP". Subtle scale-bounce (ease-out), faint camera shake on impact.
- Under it, thin caption fades: "How Pakistan's wealth got locked in land."

**[01:35] "Dead capital" tease.**
- Lower-third definition card: `DEAD CAPITAL — wealth on paper that does nothing
  productive.` White card, green keyline, slides from lower-left.

---

## PART 2 — The Ground-Truth Mechanics (2:00 - 6:00)

**[02:00] Whiteboard build-in.**
- Switch to a clean "explainer" canvas (very dark grey board). All Part 2 diagrams
  draw on with a quick write-on (Trim Paths / stroke reveal) for a hand-built feel.

**[02:45] File = promise of land.**
- Animate a booking receipt morphing into a `10 MARLA · FILE` chip.
- Cut to a society map (stylised vector grid of plots). A large green `?` pulses
  over an unassigned plot. Caption: "No fixed location yet. Decided later, by ballot."

**[03:30] The flip chain (KEY SHOT).**
- Four silhouetted buyer figures left-to-right. A file card passes between them.
- Above each handoff, the price steps up: `50 → 55 → 62 → 70 lakh`, each with a red-
  to-green `OWN +5` stamp. Under the chain, a running label: "Nothing built yet."
- Reuse this rig in Part 3 (it reverses there), so pre-comp it as `flipChain`.

**[04:15] Official vs market split screen.**
- Vertical split. Left header `OFFICIAL VALUE` (small, grey). Right `MARKET VALUE`
  (large, green). A visible gap animates open between the two numbers.

**[04:55] The three-bar cash gap (KEY CHART).**
- Bar chart, three columns growing bottom-up with stagger:
  `DC RATE` (short, grey) · `FBR VALUE` (medium, white) · `MARKET PRICE` (tall, green).
- Bracket the space between DC and Market, label it in red: `THE CASH GAP`.
- Pre-comp as `cashGapChart` (reused at 09:00). Source caption bottom-right.

**[05:35] Cash bypasses the bank.**
- A briefcase icon travels `BUYER → SELLER`. Mid-path, a bank building sits greyed
  and dimmed; the briefcase arcs around it (motion path), then drops into a home
  safe that clicks shut. Small red text: "Undocumented. Untraceable."

---

## PART 3 — The Core Conflict / Bottleneck (6:00 - 10:00)

**[06:00] Two entrepreneurs split screen.**
- Left: a factory. Right: a plot file. Persistent vertical divider for the section.

**[06:45] Factory friction stack.**
- On the factory side, stack icons dropping in with weight: tax form, high-tariff
  electricity meter, customs stamp, labour figures, gas flame with a slash. A margin
  bar on this side visibly shrinks (scale down on X).

**[07:40] Capital flees to land.**
- Wide shot: labelled money arrows curve away from `FACTORY`, `EXPORTS`, `TECH` and
  pool into a growing brown mound labelled `LAND`. The mound scales up as arrows feed it.
- Caption: "Capital misallocation."

**[08:20] Who benefits.**
- Slow push on a boardroom-window silhouette with faint plots reflected in the glass.
  Keep it suggestive and non-defamatory (no real logos, no real faces). Mood, not accusation.

**[09:00] The gap resists reform.**
- Recall `cashGapChart`. A hand-cursor grabs the short `OFFICIAL` bar and tries to
  drag it up toward `MARKET`; it springs back down with a rubber-band ease. Repeat once.

**[09:40] The chain reverses (payoff of 03:30).**
- Re-use `flipChain`, play it in reverse and stall: the last buyer is left holding
  the file, hand extended, no next buyer. Everything desaturates; a red `NO BID`
  stamp thuds over the frozen file.

## PART 4 — The Broader Macro Impact (10:00 - 13:00)

**[10:00] Zoom to national map.**
- Pull back from the single society to a stylised Pakistan map. Plots ignite as small
  dots across every major city (Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad/Rawalpindi, Faisalabad,
  Multan, Peshawar, Quetta). Density communicates scale.

**[10:45] Productive economy drains.**
- Money icons drain from `EXPORTS` and `JOBS` into the land dots. A thin stream
  labelled `FX EARNINGS` thins out and runs dry (mask reveal shrinking to zero).

**[11:30] The tax scale.**
- A balance scale: heavy `SALARIED / FORMAL` pan pressed down and taxed hard (coins
  raining), a near-weightless `REAL ESTATE WEALTH` pan floating up, barely touched.

**[12:10] Fear, not just greed.**
- A falling rupee note (looping downward drift). A crowd of small figures runs toward
  a single glowing plot for shelter. Caption: "A rational flight from a falling currency."

## PART 5 — Takeaways & Outlook (13:00 - End)

**[13:00-14:20] Three lesson cards.**
- Clean dark slate. Cards build one at a time, each with an icon and a bounce-in:
  1. `SEE THE REAL COST` (13:15) — eye + factory icon.
  2. `THE GAP IS THE SIGNAL` (13:45) — the mini three-bar chart as a glyph.
  3. `TRAPS BREAK SUDDENLY` (14:10) — a snapping-line/crack icon.

**[14:35] Return to the empty society, at night.**
- Same establishing shot as 00:00, now night: lights on across the society, still no
  people. Callback lands the theme. Slow fade.

**[14:55] End card.**
- Cut to black. Channel logo, subscribe prompt, and a "NEXT EPISODE" tease plate
  (leave a title slot for the next pillar pick).

## Reusable pre-comps (build once, reference everywhere)
- `flipChain` — the four-buyer file handoff (forward in Part 2, reversed in Part 3).
- `cashGapChart` — the DC / FBR / Market three-bar chart (Parts 2 and 3).
- `lowerThird_def` — definition card template (dark card, green keyline).
- `sourceCaption` — bottom-right citation caption; every on-screen figure gets one.
- `moneyArrow` — labelled curved-arrow rig for capital-flow shots (Parts 3 and 4).

## Global production notes
- Every hard number that appears on screen must carry a `sourceCaption`. Any figure
  marked [VERIFY] in the script must be confirmed before it is rendered into a graphic.
- No real company logos, no identifiable real individuals in the "who benefits"
  beats. Keep powerful-interest imagery abstract and non-defamatory.
- Motion language: purposeful and weighted, not bouncy or playful. This is an
  investigative business documentary.
