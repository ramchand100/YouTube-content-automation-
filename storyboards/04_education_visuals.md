# Storyboard & Motion-Graphic Blueprint — Episode 04
## "Why Poor Families Pay for a Free Education"

Timestamp-linked to `scripts/04_pakistan_education_trap.md`. Built for a solo editor
in **After Effects** (motion graphics) and **Premiere Pro** (assembly). No 3D, no
team. Structure follows the Investigative Brief (5 sections). Tone: human and calm;
let real footage and clean graphics carry it, no melodrama.

**Palette:** background near-black `#0A0A0A`; white text `#F5F5F5`; green `#1EB53A`
for the few hopeful beats (demand for education, the fixes); red `#FB5A5A` for cost,
failure, and the gaps. **Type:** bold condensed sans (Coolvetica/Archivo Black) for
numbers; clean sans (Inter) for captions and on-screen definitions.

---

## SECTION 1 — The Unsolved Reality (0:00 - 2:00)

**[00:00] Walking past the free school.**
- Footage/staged: dawn in a working-class street. A labourer walks his daughter past
  a large, locked government school and into a small private school in a rented house.
  Handheld, warm light. Play it plainly, no graphics, 0:00-0:30.

**[00:35] The core line.**
- On black: "The free school is right there. Millions of poor families walk past it."

**[01:05] The two numbers.**
- Two figures rise and hold: `PRIVATE SCHOOLS ~44% of students` (white) and
  `OUT OF SCHOOL ~26 million kids` (red). Source caption bottom-right.

**[01:40] Title card.**
- Cut to black. Type-on: **PAYING FOR FREE**, green accent on "FREE". Quiet.
  Sub-caption: "Why the poor pay to escape their own free schools."

---

## SECTION 2 — The Paper Trail (2:00 - 6:00)

**[02:00] The growth timeline.**
- An animated timeline draws private-school growth: `1982: ~3,000` climbing to
  `2015: 70,000+`. Caption: "Not planned by any government. Families voting with money."

**[02:45] The starved free system.**
- A budget bar drops to `GOV EDUCATION SPEND ~0.8% of GDP`, with a faint dashed line
  far above marked `recommended 4-6%`. Small note: "fell ~29% in one year." Source caption.

**[03:40] Inside the low-fee school ledger.**
- Zoom into one low-fee private school; a simple ledger builds: small fee in, rent +
  low teacher salaries out, volume of students making it balance. Caption: "A volume
  business built on low pay." Mark illustrative figures with a small `~` and source caption.

**[04:30] The family budget.**
- `Income Rs30,000/month`; fees for three children peel off a visible chunk, plus
  uniforms/books/transport. Caption: "One of the biggest fixed costs in the home."

**[05:15] Define shadow education.**
- `defCard`: "SHADOW EDUCATION = extra paid tuition on top of regular school." Then a
  stat: `~2 in 3 urban children attend evening tuition`. Caption: "Paying for school twice."

---

## SECTION 3 — The Field Reality (6:00 - 10:00)

**[06:00] Inside the neglected government school.**
- Footage/staged: an empty classroom, a broken fan, no water, an open register on a
  dusty desk. Let the emptiness speak.

**[06:30] Define the ghosts.**
- Two `defCard`s: "GHOST SCHOOL = exists on paper, barely functions or never opens."
  "GHOST TEACHER = on the payroll, rarely or never shows up." Then: `~25,000 ghost
  teachers · 1,000s of ghost schools`. Source caption.

**[07:20] Presence, not prestige.**
- Split: left, a government class with no teacher; right, a small private class where
  the owner replaces an absent teacher. Caption: "Parents are buying presence."

**[08:15] The tuition academy.**
- Footage: a packed evening coaching academy, whiteboard, a fee receipt. Caption:
  "A cash economy that rivals the school itself."

**[08:40] Rote and guess-papers.**
- A chalkboard of copied lines; a stack of guidebooks and "guess papers". Caption:
  "Memorize to pass, not to understand."

**[09:00] The learning result.**
- Plain bar: `Grade 5 children who can read a Grade 2 story: ~half`. A second faded bar
  for arithmetic. Caption: "Paying more, still not learning the basics." Source caption.

---

## SECTION 4 — The Systemic Domino Effect (10:00 - 13:00)

**[10:00] The national map.**
- Pull back to a map dotted with school icons, many faded/dark.

**[10:35] The future workforce.**
- `~26 million out of school` holds; caption: "plus millions inside school not learning
  = the future workforce." Tie-back note to the exports/productivity episodes.

**[11:10] The gender gap.**
- Two rows of children, boys and girls; part of the girls' row fades out. Caption:
  "Among those left out, more are girls." Source caption.

**[11:25] The widening staircase.**
- One child climbs a staircase labelled `paid school + tuition`; another is stuck at
  the bottom behind a `ghost school` block. Caption: "Inequality, locked in."

**[12:05] The shrinking budget.**
- A shrinking government education slice next to a growing crowd of children. Caption:
  "Cheap today, ruinously expensive tomorrow."

---

## SECTION 5 — The Verdict & Future Outlook (13:00 - End)

**[13:00] The question.**
- Clean slate: `IS THE PRIVATE BOOM GOOD NEWS?`

**[13:20] Two readings.**
- The same line graph labelled twice: `HOPE` (green) and `WARNING` (red). Caption:
  "Both are true."

**[13:55] The real fix.**
- A split: `FIX THE FREE SYSTEM` (end ghost schools, teachers show up, pay/train, spend
  more) vs `REGULATE QUALITY` (a floor for private schools). Keep this beat green.

**[14:30] Back to the street.**
- Return to the opening: the labourer and daughter at the private school gate. Caption:
  "The demand is not the problem. The supply is."

**[14:50] Takeaways + end card.**
- Three quiet takeaway lines (founders / employers / policy), then cut to black.
  Channel logo, subscribe, `NEXT EPISODE` tease.

## Reusable pre-comps
- `defCard` — plain-English definition card (shadow education, ghost school, ghost teacher).
- `growthTimeline` — the 1982-to-2015 private-school growth animation (Section 2).
- `budgetBar` — the education-spend-vs-recommended bar (Sections 2 and 4).
- `staircaseGap` — the paid-vs-ghost inequality staircase (Section 4).
- `sourceCaption` — bottom-right citation for every on-screen figure.

## Global production notes
- Dignity first. Show real classrooms and streets honestly; no mocking, no poverty
  spectacle. Prefer wide shots and staged recreations over identifiable children.
- Every on-screen number carries a `sourceCaption`. Any [VERIFY] figure must be
  confirmed before it is rendered.
- Reserve green for the two hopeful beats (families' demand for education; the fixes),
  so they stand out against the red of the failures.
- Calm, low score. Let the facts and the faces do the work.
