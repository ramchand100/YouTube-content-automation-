# Storyboard & Motion-Graphic Blueprint — Episode 05
## "Why a Country of 240 Million Won't Buy Insurance"

Timestamp-linked to `scripts/05_pakistan_insurance_gap.md`. Built for a solo editor
in **After Effects** (motion graphics) and **Premiere Pro** (assembly). No 3D, no
team. Structure follows the Investigative Brief (5 sections). Tone: calm and
respectful; the faith and family beats must be handled with care, never mockery.

**Palette:** background near-black `#0A0A0A`; white text `#F5F5F5`; green `#1EB53A`
for the shared pool, takaful, and the hopeful path; red `#FB5A5A` for loss, the gap,
and mis-selling. **Type:** bold condensed sans (Coolvetica/Archivo Black) for
numbers; clean sans (Inter) for captions and on-screen definitions.

---

## SECTION 1 — The Unsolved Reality (0:00 - 2:00)

**[00:00] The burnt shop.**
- Footage/staged: a small shop after an overnight fire, shutters half down, burnt
  stock, the owner in the doorway. No assessor, no paperwork. Let it sit, no graphics,
  0:00-0:30.

**[00:35] The core line.**
- On black: "Almost no one in Pakistan is insured."

**[01:05] The two numbers.**
- Rise and hold: `PAKISTAN ~0.7% of GDP` vs `WORLD AVERAGE ~6.7%`, plus a small note
  `India/China ~4%`. Source caption bottom-right.

**[01:40] Title card.**
- Cut to black. Type-on: **THE UNINSURED COUNTRY**, green accent on "UN". Quiet.
  Sub-caption: "A country full of risk, with almost no cover."

---

## SECTION 2 — The Paper Trail (2:00 - 6:00)

**[02:00] What insurance is (KEY GRAPHIC).**
- The shared-pot animation: many small figures each drop a coin into one pot; one
  figure in trouble draws from it. Pre-comp as `sharedPot` (reused in Section 5).

**[02:25] Define insurance.**
- `defCard`: "INSURANCE = many people pay a little into a shared pot, so the unlucky
  few who suffer a loss get paid from it." Then a `defCard` for "PREMIUM = the small
  amount each person pays in."

**[03:05] Tiny pool vs big pool.**
- A small pool labelled `Pakistan` next to a large one labelled `typical country`.
  Caption: "Life side dominated by one state company; general side smaller still."

**[03:50] Define the protection gap.**
- `defCard`: "PROTECTION GAP = the difference between the losses people face and the
  tiny share actually insured." Then show the gap as a wide red band.

**[04:35] The household safety net.**
- Build: a figure `the earner` + a small pile of `gold`. A shock removes the earner;
  the gold pile shrinks fast to nothing. Caption: "After that, nothing catches them."
  Tie-back note to the healthcare episode.

**[05:20] The regulator's target.**
- A modest bar nudges up: `SECP target: ~0.9% to 1.5% of GDP`. Caption: "Even that is
  a stretch, and still far below the world." Source caption.

## SECTION 3 — The Field Reality (6:00 - 10:00)

**[06:00] Three doors.**
- Three closed doors labelled `FAITH`, `TRUST`, `THE FAMILY`. They open one at a time
  through the section. Pre-comp as `threeDoors`.

**[06:25] Door one: faith.**
- Open `FAITH`. Plain, respectful explainer of the religious objection, with the three
  terms as gentle `defCard`s: "riba = interest", "gharar = too much uncertainty",
  "maysir = gambling". Keep the tone neutral and factual.

**[07:00] Takaful.**
- A `takaful` card: a shared donation pool, members helping each other. Stat bar:
  `takaful ~15% of life insurance · ~Rs100B contributions`. Green. Source caption.

**[07:50] Door two: broken trust.**
- Open `TRUST`. A bank counter, a "guaranteed savings plan" leaflet. Then the reveal:
  a premium bar where `60-80%` of year one is eaten by commission (red), customer
  underwater until `year 7`. Caption: "Sold as savings. It wasn't." Source caption.

**[08:45] Door three: the family.**
- Open `THE FAMILY`. Warm scene: family and community, a "committee" pool of hands,
  gold bangles, a remittance envelope. `defCard`: "COMMITTEE = a rotating savings pool
  among friends." Caption: "Your brother is your life insurance."

**[09:35] The three doors together.**
- All three doors open side by side. Caption: "Faith, trust, and family."

## SECTION 4 — The Systemic Domino Effect (10:00 - 13:00)

**[10:00] National view.**
- Pull back to a map; a shock ripples across it with almost nothing to absorb it.

**[10:35] The flood gap.**
- A flood scene; a thin `insured losses` sliver next to a huge `total losses` bar.
  Caption: "The loss falls on families and the state." Source caption.

**[11:25] The missing big investors.**
- Empty seats in a "capital market" where `pension funds` and `insurers` would sit.
  Caption: "No long-term local money, so big projects lean on foreign loans."

**[12:10] Callback to gold and plots.**
- Money flows into `gold` and `plots` instead of a shared pool. Caption: "No trusted
  cover, so families hoard what they can hold." Tie-back to the real-estate episode.

## Reusable pre-comps
- `sharedPot` — the coins-into-one-pot insurance explainer (Sections 2 and 5).
- `defCard` — plain-English definition card (insurance, premium, protection gap,
  riba/gharar/maysir, takaful, committee).
- `threeDoors` — the faith / trust / family doors (Section 3).
- `gapBars` — insured-vs-total loss bars (Section 4).
- `sourceCaption` — bottom-right citation for every on-screen figure.

## Global production notes
- Handle the faith beat with genuine respect. Neutral, factual framing; no imagery
  that could read as mocking religious belief. This is analysis, not commentary on faith.
- Every on-screen number carries a `sourceCaption`. Any [VERIFY] figure must be
  confirmed before it is rendered.
- Reserve green for the shared pool, takaful, and the hopeful path, so they stand out
  against the red of loss and mis-selling.
- Calm, low score. Let the mechanics carry the tension.
