# Storyboard & Motion-Graphic Blueprint — Episode 03
## "Getting Sick Can Make You Poor: Inside Pakistan's Cash Healthcare Trap"

Timestamp-linked to `scripts/03_pakistan_healthcare_cash_trap.md`. Built for a solo
editor in **After Effects** (motion graphics) and **Premiere Pro** (assembly). No 3D,
no team. Structure follows the Investigative Brief (5 sections). Tone is human and
restrained: this topic is emotional, so let real footage and simple graphics carry
it, never melodrama.

**Palette:** background near-black `#0A0A0A`; white text `#F5F5F5`; green `#1EB53A`
for the few hopeful/solution beats; red `#FB5A5A` for cost, loss, and the poverty
line. **Type:** bold condensed sans (Coolvetica/Archivo Black) for numbers; clean
sans (Inter) for captions and the on-screen definitions.

---

## SECTION 1 — The Unsolved Reality (0:00 - 2:00)

**[00:00] The cashier window.**
- Staged/stock footage: a hospital billing window at night. A man counts thin
  banknotes, pauses, takes out gold bangles to cover the rest. Shallow depth of
  field, warm practical light. Let it play, no graphics, 0:00-0:25.

**[00:30] The core line.**
- On black, type-on: "In Pakistan, you mostly pay for healthcare with your own
  money." Plain white text, slow.

**[01:00] The 11 million.**
- A large number counts up and holds: `~11,000,000 people`. Sub-caption:
  "pushed into poverty in one year, by medical bills." Source caption bottom-right.

**[01:35] Title card.**
- Cut to black. Type-on: **ONE ILLNESS AWAY**, green accent on "ONE". Quiet, no
  bombast. Thin sub-caption: "How getting sick became a way to get poor."

---

## SECTION 2 — The Paper Trail (2:00 - 6:00)

**[02:00] The pie chart shell.**
- An empty pie chart draws its outline, ready to fill. This is the section's anchor
  graphic. Pre-comp as `healthPie`.

**[02:40] Spending vs peers.**
- Two bars: `PAKISTAN ~3% of income` (short, red) vs `SIMILAR COUNTRIES ~5.5%`
  (taller, muted). Caption: "We spend little to begin with." Source caption.

**[03:20] Define "out of pocket".**
- Definition card slides in: "OUT OF POCKET = money you pay yourself, with no
  insurance or government help." Use the reusable `defCard` style (white card,
  green keyline). Every defined term in this episode uses this card.

**[03:50] Fill the pie.**
- `healthPie` fills: a large red slice `MEDICINES ~51%`, then `DOCTOR FEES ~13%`,
  `TESTS ~8%`, `TRANSPORT ~8%`. Emphasize the medicine slice with a slight pop.

**[04:40] The household example.**
- Build: `Family income Rs50,000/month`. A recurring chunk peels off labelled
  "diabetes medicine, every month, forever". Then a spike labelled "one hospital
  stay" swallows most of the bar.

**[05:00] Insurance card vs wallet.**
- Split: left, a hand slides an insurance card across a desk (other countries);
  right, a hand opens a thin wallet (Pakistan). Under it: `National health pool
  ~$10B/yr · govt pays <40%`. Source caption.

**[05:20] The Sehat Card fine print.**
- A `Sehat Card` graphic appears; a stamp lands: `INPATIENT ONLY`. Small caption:
  "covers the hospital bed, not the daily medicine." Use `defCard` for "inpatient =
  when you are admitted to a hospital bed."

---

## SECTION 3 — The Field Reality (6:00 - 10:00)

**[06:00] The pharmacy as first doctor.**
- Footage/staged: a busy neighbourhood pharmacy, people describing symptoms across
  the counter, pharmacist handing over strips. No doctor present.

**[06:30] Part-strips.**
- Close graphic/footage: a full medicine box vs a few loose tablets. Caption:
  "bought a few at a time, stopped when the money runs out." Keep it quiet and real.

**[07:20] Two systems side by side.**
- Split screen: left, a crowded bare public hospital; right, a clean cash-only
  private clinic. Overlay: `>80% of out-of-pocket money goes to private care`.

**[07:50] Rationing and rising prices.**
- A small stall with an unlicensed "doctor"; a hand-written medicine price list with
  numbers scribbled higher. Caption: "care delayed, doses cut, prices climbing."

**[08:15] Doctors leaving.**
- A departures-board style graphic: doctor silhouettes leaving, a counter ticking to
  `~5,000 in 2 years`. Caption: "trained here, working abroad." Source caption.

**[09:00] Selling what you own.**
- Restrained footage/staged: a father borrowing from a relative; gold bangles handed
  over at a jeweller. This is the emotional center; hold, do not over-score it.

---

## SECTION 4 — The Systemic Domino Effect (10:00 - 13:00)

**[10:00] One family to many.**
- Pull back from a single home to a wide map; thousands of small household icons light
  up across the country.

**[10:30] The poverty line jump.**
- A bar at `20.5%` rises to `27%` when a red block labelled `medical bills` is added.
  Caption: "~13 million more people, made poor by illness." Source caption.

**[11:15] The shrinking plate.**
- A household budget graphic: a plate of food shrinks as a medicine bill grows.
  Caption: "medicine crowds out food, worst for the poorest."

**[12:00] The trap loop.**
- A simple loop diagram turning: `sick → sell assets → poorer → weaker → sick`.

**[12:35] The squeezed budget.**
- A small government-budget pie: big slices for `DEBT` and `DEFENCE`, a tiny sliver
  for `HEALTH`. Caption: "little tax collected, little room to spend."

---

## SECTION 5 — The Verdict & Future Outlook (13:00 - End)

**[13:00] The question.**
- Clean slate: `CAN THIS BE FIXED?`

**[13:20] Half a shield.**
- The `Sehat Card` returns; a shield draws over a hospital icon but stops short of a
  pharmacy icon. Caption: "covers the hospital, not the daily medicine."

**[13:55] What insurance actually is.**
- Reusable `defCard` plus animation: many people drop small coins into one shared
  pot; one sick person draws from it. This is the episode's key idea, made simple.

**[14:10] The three fixes.**
- Three icons in a row: a phone (telemedicine), a shared-pot (micro-insurance), a
  pharmacy chain. Keep these green, the hopeful beat.

**[14:30] Back to the queue.**
- Return to the opening cashier window, now a wider shot: a long queue of families
  waiting to pay. Caption: "the family carries the risk, alone."

**[14:50] Takeaways + end card.**
- Three quiet takeaway lines build (founders / employers / policy), then cut to
  black. Channel logo, subscribe, `NEXT EPISODE` tease.

## Reusable pre-comps
- `healthPie` — the out-of-pocket spending pie (Section 2).
- `defCard` — plain-English definition card (used for every defined term).
- `povertyBar` — the 20.5% to 27% poverty-line jump (Section 4).
- `sharedPot` — the coins-into-one-pot insurance explainer (Sections 2 and 5).
- `sourceCaption` — bottom-right citation for every on-screen figure.

## Global production notes
- Emotional restraint. Real footage carries the human weight; graphics stay clean and
  factual. No sad-piano manipulation; a low, quiet score.
- Every on-screen number carries a `sourceCaption`. Any [VERIFY] figure must be
  confirmed before it is rendered.
- Dignity first: show hardship without turning real people into props. No identifiable
  faces of real patients without consent; prefer hands, wide shots, and staged recreations.
- Green is reserved for the few solution beats so the one hopeful section stands out.
