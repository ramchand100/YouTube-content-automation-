# Storyboard & Motion-Graphic Blueprint — Episode 02
## "Why Pakistan Still Pays to Move Money When Raast Is Free"

Timestamp-linked to `scripts/02_raast_vs_card_networks.md`. Built for a solo editor
in **After Effects** (motion graphics) and **Premiere Pro** (assembly). No 3D, no
team required. Structure follows the Investigative Brief (5 sections).

**Palette:** background near-black `#0A0A0A`; primary text white `#F5F5F5`; accent
green `#1EB53A` for Raast, "free", and positive flows; alert red `#FB5A5A` for
fees, leakage, and cost. **Type:** bold condensed sans (Coolvetica / Archivo Black)
for numbers; clean sans (Inter) for captions. Keep Raast green and cards red as a
consistent visual language throughout.

---

## SECTION 1 — The Unsolved Reality (0:00 - 2:00)

**[00:00] The unused QR sticker.**
- Footage or staged shot: a small shop counter, a faded Raast QR sticker curling at
  the edge, a customer paying cash. Shallow depth of field, warm practical lighting.
- Let it play naturally 0:00-0:20, no graphics. Establish the "ordinary counter" mood.

**[00:25] The two numbers.**
- Overlay two figures side by side on a darkened plate: `P2P: Rs11.3 TRILLION` (green)
  vs `P2M: Rs17 BILLION` (small, red). Animate both counting up; let the size
  difference land visually. Source caption bottom-right.

**[01:05] Still the default.**
- Quick cuts: a card swiped through a POS machine (red accent), then cash sliding
  across a counter. Label stamps in: `STILL THE DEFAULT`.

**[01:35] Title card.**
- Cut to black. Type-on: **WHY PAKISTAN PAYS TO PAY**, condensed, green on "PAYS".
  Subtle scale-settle, faint low bass hit. Thin sub-caption fades: "A free rail, and
  a country that still pays to move its own money."

---

## SECTION 2 — The Paper Trail (2:00 - 6:00)

**[02:00] The Rs2,000 receipt (through-line).**
- Establish a single receipt graphic: `PURCHASE: Rs2,000`. This element stays on
  screen, in various states, for the whole section. Pre-comp as `receipt2000`.

**[02:20] The MDR slice.**
- A green slice peels off the receipt, turns red, labelled `MDR ~2% = Rs40`. The
  shop's take visibly drops from Rs2,000 to Rs1,960.

**[02:55] The Rs40 splits three ways.**
- The red Rs40 chip divides into three labelled pieces with a staggered split:
  `INTERCHANGE → issuing bank` (largest), `SCHEME FEE → Visa / Mastercard`, `ACQUIRER`.
- Pre-comp as `feeSplit` (referenced again conceptually in Section 3's bank beat).

**[03:45] Dollars leave vs money stays.**
- Contrast panel. Left: a Visa/Mastercard chip with a small dollar sign flying off
  the map (red). Right: a PayPak chip with the rupee staying inside a Pakistan outline
  (neutral/green). Caption: "Domestic only, but the money stays home."

**[04:20] Raast near-zero.**
- The `receipt2000` again, now with a Raast QR badge. A near-flat line animates:
  `COST ≈ Rs0` in green. Deliberately anticlimactic, that is the point.

**[04:45] Merchant annualization (KEY MATH).**
- A daily counter spins: `Rs100,000/day card sales` → `~Rs2,000/day MDR` →
  `~Rs60,000/month`. End on the line `= a salary` for punch. Source caption.

**[05:00] The multiplier.**
- `receipt2000` multiplies across the frame into a grid, tag `x billions of
  transactions`, then a national ticker: `9.1B txns · Rs612T (FY24-25)`.

**[05:35] The subsidy.**
- Coins flow from a `STATE` node to a `MERCHANT` node, tagged `+0.5% or Rs100 per QR
  sale`. Caption: "Paying people to take the cheaper deal." Let the irony sit.

---

## SECTION 3 — The Field Reality (6:00 - 10:00)

**[06:00] The shopkeeper and two ledgers.**
- Return to the counter, now focused on the shopkeeper. Two physical ledgers sit
  under the till. Keep it observational, not accusatory.

**[06:25] Trace vs no-trace.**
- A cash note drops and vanishes with no residue. A digital payment drops and leaves
  a glowing record stamped `VISIBLE TO FBR` (red). This is the section's core image.

**[07:15] The cash mountain.**
- `Rs7 TRILLION` currency-in-circulation graphic building into a mound, caption
  `~13% of GDP in cash`. Beside it, thin card stats fade in: `<49M cards · <10% of
  people · 3.53 POS uses/card/yr vs ~66.5 EU`.

**[07:35] The cash supply chain.**
- Three nodes: `DISTRIBUTOR → SHOPKEEPER → CUSTOMER`, cash bundles passing along the
  whole line, everything glowing "CASH". When one node flips to a documented rail, a
  red mismatch flag pops between recorded sales and unrecorded purchases.

**[08:00] Settlement timing.**
- Split clock graphic: `CARD → T+1, minus fees` vs `CASH / RAAST → instant`. A small
  working-capital gauge dips on the card side.

**[08:05-08:55] The bank incentive.**
- Shift to a bank building. `INTERCHANGE FEE → bank income` arrows glow green into the
  bank. Then a Raast QR is placed on the counter and the bank's arrow goes flat/grey,
  labelled `Raast = Rs0 to the bank`. Reuse the `feeSplit` idea to show what the bank
  loses. A shopkeeper is handed a QR sticker and shrugs; a POS terminal stacks up beside it.

---

## SECTION 4 — The Systemic Domino Effect (10:00 - 13:00)

**[10:00] Pull back to national view.**
- From the counter to a stylised Pakistan map, payment flows lighting across cities.

**[10:30] FX leakage.**
- Dollars peel off card transactions and fly off the edge of the map, tagged
  `FX LEAKAGE` (red). Contrast a domestic rail keeping rupees inside the outline.

**[11:05] The tax base.**
- Two pools: a small `DOCUMENTED / SALARIED` pool taxed hard (coins raining), a vast
  `CASH ECONOMY` pool barely touched. Caption ties Raast to documentation.

**[11:35] Monetary transmission.**
- The SBP building with a policy-rate dial. A large greyed cash pool sits outside the
  banking system, unreachable by the dial's ripples. Caption: "Cash blunts the lever."

**[11:50] The leapfrog.**
- A landline crossed out, a mobile phone rising, relabelled `cards → instant rails`.
  Communicates: Pakistan may skip a card-heavy economy entirely.

**[12:30] The consumer at the end.**
- A customer at a till, a small `+2%` tag floating over marked-up prices. The cost
  lands on the person at the counter either way.

---

## SECTION 5 — The Verdict & Future Outlook (13:00 - End)

**[13:00] The question.**
- Clean slate, single line: `WILL THE FREE RAIL WIN?`

**[13:15] The two-line chart.**
- Simple line chart: `P2P` rising steeply (green), `P2M` crawling off a near-zero base
  (amber). The gap is the story.

**[13:50] Three friction blocks.**
- Three blocks stack and lock: `TAX VISIBILITY` (largest), `BANK FEE INCOME`,
  `HABIT & INFRASTRUCTURE`. The subsidy graphic from 05:35 briefly recalls as the "tell".

**[14:30] Documented vs cash economy.**
- The two ledgers from Section 3 return, relabelled `DOCUMENTED ECONOMY` vs
  `CASH ECONOMY`, facing off. This is the real contest, stated plainly.

**[14:55] Resolution callback.**
- Return to the opening shop counter. This time the QR is scanned, a phone shows a
  completed Raast payment (green tick). Hold, then to black. End card: channel logo,
  subscribe, and a `NEXT EPISODE` tease plate.

## Reusable pre-comps
- `receipt2000` — the Rs2,000 receipt through-line (Section 2).
- `feeSplit` — the Rs40 three-way fee split (Sections 2 and 3).
- `cashChain` — distributor/shopkeeper/customer cash flow (Section 3).
- `mapFlows` — national payment-flow map (Section 4).
- `sourceCaption` — bottom-right citation caption for every on-screen figure.

## Global production notes
- Keep the visual grammar consistent: **green = Raast / free / stays home**, **red =
  card fees / leakage / cost**. Do not mix these up mid-video.
- Every on-screen rupee figure carries a `sourceCaption`. Any [VERIFY] figure must be
  confirmed before it is rendered into a graphic.
- No real bank logos or identifiable individuals. Keep institutions abstract.
- Motion language: measured and analytical, not flashy. This is an investigation.
